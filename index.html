Moonline

One line a day. Then it's sealed.

Moonline is a journal for iPhone that takes a minute a night. Write a few words before bed. Tomorrow you can still add to them; after that the day is sealed, kept exactly as you wrote it. Every night you write lights a moon on your calendar.

Common questions

Can I edit or delete an old entry?
No. Today stays open, and yesterday until noon; older days are sealed on purpose. You can clear the whole journal in Settings › Journal › Delete all entries.

Where are my entries stored?
On your iPhone. With Moonline+ and iCloud sync turned on, they also sync through your own private iCloud. Moonline has no servers.

What does Moonline+ add?
Every entry you've ever written (the free version shows the last 30 days), the "A year ago today" notification, and private iCloud sync. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

I bought Moonline+ but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings › Moonline+ and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

My entries aren't syncing.
Check that you're signed in to iCloud, that iCloud has free storage, and that iCloud sync is on in Moonline's Settings on each device.
